    🎨 AQI Scale

    Standard AQI categories
    AQICategoryMeaning
    0–50GoodAir quality is satisfactory.
    51–100ModerateAcceptable; very sensitive people may be affected.
    101–150Unhealthy for Sensitive GroupsChildren, elderly, and people with lung/heart disease are at risk.
    151–200UnhealthyEveryone may begin to feel health effects.
    201–300Very UnhealthyHealth alert: serious effects for everyone.
    301–500HazardousEmergency conditions; avoid all outdoor exposure.

    💚 Everyday Clean-Air Tips

    • Check the AQI before outdoor exercise; plan activity for cleaner hours.
    • Keep windows closed during peak pollution; ventilate when AQI is low.
    • Use an N95/FFP2 mask outdoors when AQI is above 150.
    • Use public transport, carpool, or cycle to cut urban emissions.
    • Stay hydrated and avoid burning waste or smoking indoors.

    🏙️ About

    Urban Air Quality Assistant is a lightweight, rule-based web app that helps citizens understand air pollution and make safer outdoor decisions. It runs fully offline in your browser with no API keys, no tracking, and no data sent to any server.

    Health guidance is general information, not medical advice. Consult a doctor for personal health concerns.
